Editorial standards

How we assess policy

The aim is not to tell you what to think. It is to make evidence, trade-offs and uncertainty easier to examine.

01

Separate promise from delivery

Distinguish announcements, legislation, funding, implementation outputs and real-world outcomes.

02

Show competing arguments fairly

Present left- and right-leaning arguments as perspectives to test, not labels that determine truth.

03

Attribute alternatives

Attribute party proposals to the party publishing them; do not present them as enacted Government policy.

04

Make uncertainty visible

State when evidence is incomplete, outdated, not comparable or insufficient to support a conclusion.

This is a developing research resource, not an independent audit, official Government publication or endorsement of any party. Some party-position summaries and source references remain subject to original-document verification.
